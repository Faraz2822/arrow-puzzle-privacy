Arrow Puzzle Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how the Arrow Puzzle mobile game ("the game"), developed by Faraz2822 ("we"), handles information. The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and those ads are how it earns money.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts, and we don't ask for your name, email address or other contact details.
  • We don't run servers for the game, and we don't receive or store your personal information.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • Google AdMob collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device so it can remember your progress:

  • Your current level and coin balance
  • The skins you own or are trying, and the skin you have selected
  • Your sound and vibration settings

This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

Advertising and Google AdMob

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner, full-screen and rewarded ads. Google may collect and use information from your device, which can include:

  • The device's advertising ID and other online identifiers
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as device type and operating system
  • How you interact with ads, along with diagnostic and performance data

Google uses this information for purposes such as showing ads, personalizing them where permitted, measuring their performance and preventing fraud. Google, not us, controls how it handles this information. You can read more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices

  • Ad consent: where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your choice before ads are personalized. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy choices in the game's settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings. You can also control ad personalization in your Google Ad Settings.
  • Stopping all collection: uninstalling the game stops it from collecting or sharing any further information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as asking to access or delete it. Because we don't hold your personal information, requests about advertising data are best made to Google using the links above. You're also welcome to contact us with any questions.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the game's features or ad partners change. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
